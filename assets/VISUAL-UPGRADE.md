# Realistic visual refresh

## Assessment

The original kit contains 16 stylized, low-poly assets with solid-color PBR materials. Its faceted tree crowns, layered cone pines, simplified people and wildlife, and bright painted equipment establish a playful miniature aesthetic. The runtime reinforces that look with flat-shaded auxiliary meshes, polygonal hill and cloud geometry, solid-color terrain, and concentric target disks. More polygons alone would not resolve this; material detail, lighting, proportions, terrain treatment, and interface styling also need to work together.

## Delivered environment and surfaces

- A generated photographic Colorado mountain panorama replaces the polygon hill and cloud backdrop. The 2172 × 724 PNG is used as a distant cylindrical vista and a source for filtered environmental reflections. The saved prompt and provenance are in `textures/prompts.md`.
- Five independent, seamless surface sets provide turf, gravel, granite, bark, and sand. Each includes a 1024 × 1024 color map and a matching bump map. Short grass fibers, small aggregate, mineral flecks, vertical bark fissures, and fine sand variation replace flat colors.
- World-space terrain UVs maintain material density when the range grows. Organic target greens and grass fringes replace the geometric bullseyes. The playable fairway remains compatible with the existing physics.
- Instanced verge grass and shader-driven flag movement add subtle environmental motion. Rounded auxiliary geometry, textured stones, net material, atmospheric fog, warm sunlight, ACES tone mapping, and higher-resolution shadows support the new direction.
- Dark evergreen interface panels, warm brass accents, restrained typography, and reduced corner radii establish a mature golf-club presentation.

## Delivered model kit

All 16 existing GLBs were replaced: a detailed commercial picker, adult-proportion golfer, organic branching trees with textured foliage, more natural wildlife anatomy, and timber-and-steel range architecture. Asset identifiers, wheel/steering/collector pivots, placement anchors, and animation names are preserved. The current 17-model kit, including the visitor cart, totals **8,858,024 bytes**. Every GLB passes the Khronos validator with **zero errors and zero warnings**, including the additional runtime compatibility checks.

The tractor has 42,836 triangles and 37 meshes, reduced from 88,708 triangles in the first detailed draft. The pine has 3,532 triangles and the broadleaf tree 1,858, with two material groups each for efficient instancing. Dense needle branchlets and alpha-to-coverage retain pine canopy coverage at range distances. Editable Blender source, the reproducible generator, embedded source textures and refreshed collection/tractor/wildlife renders are included.

## Source exports and browser payload

The complete PNG source surface maps total **14,520,240 bytes**. Their matching browser WebP maps total **5,023,660 bytes**, a **65.4% reduction**. Albedos use quality 92 and bump maps quality 95, both with WebP encoder method 6. Source PNGs and the photographic panorama are retained.

Rebuild surfaces with `python assets/tools/generate_surfaces.py` after installing Pillow. The generator uses fixed seeds, periodic noise sampling, and wrapping surface primitives. Preview all materials in `previews/surface-materials.jpg`.

## Golfer and visitor follow-up

The golfers now face the opposite direction for a right-handed stance. The tee and hitting strip sit on the left side of each mat so the golfer fits inside the bay. The three-second swing uses articulated shoulders, elbows, wrists, torso, hips, knees and a rising trail heel. Hands reach almost head height on both sides and the shaft wraps across the shoulders. Address and impact club-head positions meet the tee; impact occurs at exactly 34/24 seconds and the runtime releases the ball at that pose. `previews/golfer-swing.png` shows six poses.

Visitors have detailed two-seat resort golf carts with seated drivers, wheel pivots and rear golf bags. Carts arrive along the access lane, park behind the bay, and remain while the golfer plays. Departing golfers walk back, board, reverse into the lane, and drive away. New arrivals wait until their boarding walk completes before consuming a ball. The additional standalone `models/golf-cart.glb` is generated by the packaged `code/golf-cart.ts` and `tools/export-golf-cart.mjs`; all 17 exports validate without errors or warnings.

Browser saves preserve the full live shift, including airborne and spilled balls, golfer swing/walk timing, moving visitor carts, wildlife, randomized obstacle placement, upgrades and economy. Named saves are independent of autosave; reloading offers a paused continuation.

## Practical limits

The new direction combines a photographic backdrop with authored procedural geometry and materials. The surfaces are reproducible material textures rather than scanned photogrammetry. The panorama is a distant environment image, so it cannot provide close-range mountain parallax. Wildlife and golfer motion remain compatible with the game's transform-based animation system. High-quality mode adds verge grass and shadows; the existing lower-quality path remains available for constrained devices.

The panorama, material contact sheet, model renders, desktop game, and mobile welcome and play layouts were visually inspected. All four animation clips produced changing poses in Three.js. All 16 assets loaded and the application reported no runtime errors. Runtime integration exercised collection of 19 balls and a depot return that increased reserve by exactly 19, plus 300-yard expansion, 12 bays, a second depot, helper cart, and cart upgrades. The revised cage and hopper mesh were subsequently inspected in the final runtime. Menu, chase and overhead cameras, graphics quality, and wind settings were exercised through the browser. At 390 × 844 the touch controls and map do not overlap and the page has no horizontal overflow.

The 23 automated gameplay and persistence tests, production build, and all 17 GLB validations pass. Real-time frame rate was not benchmarked: the collaborative browser throttles background animation frames. The expanded high-quality scene is substantially more demanding; Low disables shadows and verge grass. Browser automation intermittently disconnected but recovered, and the game was left loaded in the collaborative preview. Current verification is recorded in `game-validation.json`; the older Babylon.js report remains a historical milestone.
